## Entry criteria

- [ ] G8 release record is approved and identifies an immutable source,
  artifact, SBOM, lockfile, VVR, screenshots and label/IFU archive.
- [ ] Attachment sources `00`–`09` contain no unresolved placeholder.
- [ ] Generated PDFs match their approved Markdown sources and have recorded
  cryptographic hashes.
- [ ] Required clinical, quality, security/privacy, French and counsel reviews
  are signed by named people with authority and independence documented.

## Independent audit

- [ ] Every version, full/short commit, build and artifact hash is consistent.
- [ ] Every screenshot is legible, uncropped and traced to the frozen build,
  approved device/browser and test record.
- [ ] Feature, workflow and architecture summaries match both the executable
  artifact and approved REG-001/ARC-001.
- [ ] Intended use, limitations, warnings and claims match REG-002 in substance
  across the package; no text implies approval, clearance or authorization.
- [ ] Equations, units, examples and results match CALC-001 and independently
  verified VVR-001 evidence; anomalies/deviations are disclosed and approved.
- [ ] RMF-001/TRC-001 disclose immediate-use severity, verified controls,
  residual uncertainties and excluded uses without minimizing criterion 3.
- [ ] The four-criterion analysis preserves criterion 3 ambiguity and does not
  claim that “calculator,” local operation or visible equations are dispositive.
- [ ] Fallback classification uses current counsel-verified rules/guidance and
  treats every statutory pin-cite as a submission-date verification field.
- [ ] Architecture/network/storage/offline claims match exact-build evidence,
  including all build/runtime services and failure paths.
- [ ] English and French safety meaning is clinically equivalent and the
  translation/review trail is complete.
- [ ] Deferred clinical-release work is described as deferred, not completed;
  classification support is not presented as authorization.
- [ ] Legal manufacturer/contact, authority, IP/licensing conclusion,
  signatures, dates, filenames and attachment list are complete.
- [ ] COR-001 records the then-effective routing instruction, URL, retrieval and
  effective dates, including the 2026-12-14 transition decision.
